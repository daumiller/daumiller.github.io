ruby

 ruby's been my beefy workhorse for years. her water cooler is loud, but she's strong and dependable. she has a completely custom 3D printed case.
  • Hackintosh 11.6 Big Sur
  • Core i7
  • 64 GiB RAM

lapis

 lapis is my beautiful Blue & White Power Mac G3. i've been trying to make her my primary machine lately. she is incredibly fast and responsive, unburdened by bloat.
  • Mac OS 9.2.2
  • PowerPC G3 350 MHz
  • 384 MiB RAM (for now)

peridot

 peri is my teeny little chromebook. she was meant to be a temporary portable until i could afford something nice again, but she has been much more useful than i anticipated. her screen can flip all the way around, she's got a handy little stylus, and she's pretty good at running linux things (from within the nasty google environment that she was cursed with).
  • ChromeOS
  • Celeron N4120
  • 4 GiB RAM

jasper

 jasper is my moto g stylus. much like lapis, she's also afflicted by the curse of google, but still manages to do a fine job. she's handy for mobile SSHing, and anything else you'd want a pocket computer for. she's due for a fancy folding keyboard someday soon.
  • Android
  • Snapdragon 6G3
  • 8 GiB RAM

xp-lex

 this guy is an older Dell Optiplex with a C2D. he's setup as a pure XP machine, and is a beast at it. he crushes Visual Studio 6/.NET, Paint Shop Pro, old windows games, and even has a modern-website-capable browser (supermium). he'll soon have a some hand-me-downs that get him more RAM capacity.
  • Windows XP SP3
  • Core2Duo
  • 1 GiB RAM

mmx dude

 mmx dude is a DOS/Windows 3.1/95/98 specialist. he runs all of the great classic stuff, off of a CF disk. he currently inhabits a skeleton of a case, but he'll have a proper beige box someday.
  • triple boot DOS+3.1/95/98
  • Pentium MMX 233 MHz
  • 256 MiB RAM

starserve

 starserve slings video to any device that hits our local network. she's got roku and browser clients all over the house streaming from her. she works hard, and does an amazing job given her processing limits.
  • raspbian
  • Raspberry Pi 2B
  • 1 GiB RAM
